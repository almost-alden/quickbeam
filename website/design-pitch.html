Comparison round

Remix the strongest parts.

Four cross-combinations tested against the same mobile journey. The recommendation keeps the strongest sender model, recipient moment, and trust cue from three different concepts.

RECOMMENDED · COUCHDROP × PRIMETIME × HEARTH

Watchpost / "The message becomes a premiere"

Couchdrop's contact-first compose, Primetime's full-bleed recipient show card, and Hearth's “nothing installs” trust line. Palette: midnight ink, message blue, marquee amber, warm paper, sent green. Type: Nunito sender UI, Oswald recipient headlines, Inter utility. Strongest full-journey combination.

SIGNAL × COUCHDROP

Beamline / "Fast enough to feel like sending"

Signal's compact three-step system with Couchdrop's people-first order and thread-like send history. Keep violet as identity, use message blue only for routing and cyan for success. Outfit + Inter. Best low-risk evolution of Quickbeam.

HEARTH × PRIMETIME

Marquee / "A little occasion at home"

Hearth's warm paper and reassuring setup with Primetime's event language and recipient card. Persimmon replaces on-air red; cobalt stays the action color. Fraunces + Oswald sparingly, DM Sans for controls. Emotional without becoming cute.

SIGNAL × HEARTH

Quickbeam Home / "Clear, calm, on the TV"

Signal's hierarchy and status model, Hearth's language and setup guidance. Ink, violet, parchment, coral, status cyan. Outfit + DM Sans. Best if the name and beam equity stay while recipient anxiety becomes the design priority.

What Johnny is really choosing

  • Quickbeam as an instrument, a message between people, or a television premiere.
  • Evolution of an existing recognizable look or a full rebrand.
  • Technical confidence or emotional reassurance as the first impression.
  • One design must work across the whole phone journey: send, receive, setup, a clearly labeled sender-side ad slot, and subscription upsell.
