How it works

Three steps. Zero installs. If they can tap a link, they can do this.

1

You send a link

Paste any YouTube, Netflix, Disney+, or other streaming link into Quickbeam. We turn it into a magic link and text it to them.

2

They open it at home

They tap the link on their phone while on home Wi-Fi. Quickbeam finds their Roku on the network — nothing to install, nothing to pair.

3

They tap their TV — it plays

One tap and the right app opens to the right show or movie. Movie night starts instead of troubleshooting.
