Android / ChromeOS

Install it, then share from any app

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu → “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).
  3. Open YouTube, Chrome, or your messages, tap Share — Quickbeam now appears as a target.
  4. Shared links land right here for preview.

No files are downloaded for v1 — install just adds the icon and the share target.

iPhone / iPad

The honest iOS story

iOS does not let web apps appear in the share sheet — that's an Apple platform limit, not something we can work around. Two paths:

Now (free, no app): add the “Send to Quickbeam” Shortcut. It shows up in every app's share menu and hands the link straight to this page:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  2. Rename it “Send to Quickbeam”, tap ⓘ → Show in Share Sheet, and under Share Sheet types keep only URLs checked.
  3. Add action “URL Encode” → set its input to Shortcut Input.
  4. Add action “Text” and type https://HOST/share?url= then insert the URL Encoded result right after the =.
  5. Add action “Open URLs” and set it to the Text above. Done — it now appears under Shortcuts in any share sheet.

Also works today: Add to Home Screen from Safari for one-tap launching, plus the paste box above.

Later beta: a tiny native iOS app with a real share-sheet extension — the only way to get a first-class Quickbeam icon in the iOS share sheet. That needs an Apple Developer membership, so it's a later milestone, not this home test.
