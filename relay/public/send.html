Welcome to Couchbeam! ⚡️

Couchbeam is a simple, lightweight utility that lets you send video links to a friend's phone — they open the link, pair their own Roku TV, and cast. Completely free, with no accounts or sign-ins needed.

1

Get them set up: they open your magic link on their phone (on the same Wi-Fi as their Roku) and pair their TV once.

2

Add Contact: Under "Quick Send", add your contact's name, plus their phone or email for direct delivery.

3

Quick Send: Paste a video URL, tap their contact card, and send it! They open the link on their phone and pair their own TV. Phone-to-Roku launching is experimental and unverified until tested on real hardware.
