How it works

1

Share a link. From YouTube, Netflix, or another supported app, share the video to Couchbeam — or paste the link.

2

Preview it. Couchbeam shows what it understood before anything happens. Nothing plays until you say so.

3

Tap play. Open the link on a phone on the same home Wi-Fi as the Roku, and the video starts on the TV.

Put it in your share sheet

Android: install Couchbeam from your browser menu, and it appears as a share target in any app — YouTube, Chrome, your messages.

iPhone / iPad: iOS doesn't let web apps into the share sheet, so we made a one-tap Shortcut instead — it shows up in every app's share menu and hands the link to Couchbeam. A native share extension is planned for the later beta.

Home-test beta — good to know

  • Free demo. Everything here is free while we test at home.
  • Experimental compatibility. Service deep-linking is best-effort and still being verified on real Roku hardware.
  • Same Wi-Fi required. The phone that taps play must be on the same home Wi-Fi as the Roku TV.
  • No guarantees yet. We can't promise every streaming service deep-links correctly — the preview shows you what we understood first.
  • Beta testers: the streaming-service test harness walks through all 24 services and records what works on your hardware.
