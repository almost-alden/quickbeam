Read this first

  • Three honest states. Content deep link candidate = the link format is a plausible deep-link input, still unproven. App launch only = the Roku app ignores deep-link params, so only launching the app can be tested. Parser fixture / needs live validation = we only know the parser handles the URL shape; the page itself may be stale.
  • “Needs live link.” Web test URLs from our unit tests are parser fixtures, not proof the page is current. Use the service's official home/search link, find a real title, and share its link.
  • Results stay on this device. Everything you record lives in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — you choose when and where to share via the export buttons below.
  • Experimental. Launching the Roku from this page's HTTPS hosting is unverified on real hardware. Same home Wi-Fi required.

🤖 Android testers

Install Couchbeam from your browser menu — it becomes a share target. In any app (YouTube, Chrome, messages), share the video → Couchbeam → the link opens at /share?url=… for preview.

🍎 iPhone / iPad testers

iOS won't put web apps in the share sheet. Add Couchbeam to your Home Screen, copy the video link, and paste it at /share. For a share-sheet shortcut, install the “Send to Couchbeam” Shortcut (recipe in docs/ios-shortcut.md in the repo).
